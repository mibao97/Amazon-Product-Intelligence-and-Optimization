<b>Business Problems:</b>
 E-commerce marketplaces face constant challenges in balancing pricing strategies, inventory management, and product performance.
 
<ul></ul>
This project aims to:
<ul>
  <li>Optimize pricing and discount strategies
  <li> Improve product quality & discoverability
  <li> Identify high-performing vs underperforming products
  <li> Support data-driven merchandising decisions</ul>

​
<b>Key Insight:<b>

1. Inventory & Reviews<ul>
    <li> Products with higher total reviews tend to have higher in-stock rates
    <li> Products with higher total reviews show lower out-of-stock rates
 -> Insight: Product availability is strongly correlated with customer engagement</ul>

2. Pricing vs Stock<ul>
    <li> Higher-priced products tend to have lower stock levels
    <li> Smaller price variation within brands suggests consistent pricing strategies
    <li> Uneven price distribution across categories:
    <li> Some categories range widely (e.g., max ~$1000 vs min ~$100)
-> Insight: Premium products are more supply-constrained</ul>

4. Discount Behavior<ul>
    <li> Discount is not strongly correlated with stock levels
    <li> Cheaper products receive higher discount percentages
    <li> Higher-end categories (Electronics, Beauty): Lower discounts
    <li> Lower-end categories (Books, Toys, Fashion, Sports, Automotive): Higher discounts
-> Insight: Discounting is driven more by category positioning than inventory pressure</ul>

5. Discount Timing Trends<ul>
    <li> Discounts follow a wave pattern throughout the month:
    <li> High at the beginning
    <li> Decrease mid-month
    <li> Increase again toward the end
-> Insight: This reflects consumer psychology tactics, creating urgency and “fear of missing out” (FOMO)</ul>

7. Customer Behavior Insights<ul>
    - Customers tend to:
      <li> Purchase products from the same brand together
      <li> Perceive bundles as higher value
    - Opportunity:
      <li> Promote brand-based bundles
      <li> Offer combo deals to increase average order value (AOV)</ul>

Business Recommendations
1. Inventory Optimization<ul>
    <li> Ensure high-review products stay in stock
    <li> Use reviews as a proxy for demand forecasting</ul>
   
2. Smart Pricing Strategy<ul>
    <li> Ensure high-review products stay in stock 
    <li> Maintain consistent pricing within brands
    <li> Re-evaluate extreme price gaps across categories</ul>

3. Discount Optimization<ul>
     <li> Apply higher discounts to lower-tier categories
     <li> Use targeted discounts instead of blanket promotions</ul>

4. Strategic Promotion Timing<ul>
    <li> Leverage end-of-month discount spikes
    <li>Create campaigns around:
         “Deals are back” || 
         “Limited-time offers”</ul>
      
5. Bundle & Cross-Selling Strategy<ul>
    <li> Recommend same-brand product combinations
    <li> Offer bundle discounts to increase conversion</ul>

6. Boost Underperforming Products<ul>
    <li> Promote low-performing or overstocked items
    <li> Improve visibility and discoverability
    <li> Pair them with high-performing products</ul>
